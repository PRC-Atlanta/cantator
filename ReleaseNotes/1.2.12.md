## Services

- Select an item and its **File** cell becomes a search box. Type part of a song's title or file name; matching playbacks show under it. Pick one with the arrow keys and Return, or click it.
- Right-click an item for its actions: **Use** a suggestion, **Preview on Cue Output**, **Make Local**, **Change Track** or **Clear Match**, **Needs a Track**, **Add Item Below**, **Move Up** and **Down**, and **Delete Item**.
- On a trackpad, swipe left with two fingers on an item to show its **Delete** button. Swipe right to put it away.
- Items from Planning Center can now be deleted. The next **Refresh** brings them back as Planning Center has them, matched like any new item. Items added in Cantator stay deleted.
