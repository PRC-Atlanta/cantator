## Duplicates

- Copies of the same playback, such as an MP3 in Downloads and the WAV in Drive, or "Way Maker (1).mp3", are found on every scan. Cantator keeps one and moves the rest to **Excluded** as "Duplicate of" the one it kept. The copy that stays is the one a service uses or you chose, then the one with your end point, markers, tags or notes, then one on this Mac, then a WAV or AIFF.
- Files with the same name count as copies when their length matches, or, for a Drive file not downloaded yet, its size. A different version, such as "Full Mix" and "Instrumental" or two keys, is never treated as a copy.
- **Duplicates** in the Library sidebar lists tracks that only look like the same song. Select the one to use and choose **Keep This One** to exclude the others.

## Make All Local

- **Make All Local** in the Library toolbar copies every playback that is only in the cloud into ~/Music/Cantor. Stems, parts and excluded copies are never downloaded.
